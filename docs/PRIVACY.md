# StudentCareer AI — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 11, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 11, 2026

## 1. Introduction

StudentCareer AI ("we", "our", or "the Platform") is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and data sovereignty of students, university graduates, and job applicants who use our career automation services.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we process it to match and apply for career opportunities, our use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) models, and your legal rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and applicable international privacy laws.

---

## 2. Personal Data We Collect

To discover relevant internships, score job eligibility, tailor CVs, and prepare application packages, we collect the following categories of personal information:

1. **Identity & Contact Information:** Full name, email address, phone number, location/timezone, LinkedIn profile URL, GitHub handle, and personal portfolio links.
2. **Academic & Education History:** Enrolled university, degree title, major/minor, cumulative GPA, expected graduation date, relevant coursework, and academic honors.
3. **Professional & Work History:** Previous internship and job titles, company names, employment dates, responsibilities, projects, and verifiable achievements.
4. **Skills & Qualifications:** Technical competencies, programming languages, software frameworks, tools, and language proficiencies.
5. **Master Curriculum Vitae (CV):** Raw markdown, text, HTML, or PDF resumes uploaded by the student.
6. **Application Q&A & Cover Letters:** Answers generated for job applications, customized cover letters, and candidate-provided screening answers.
7. **Job Search & Application History:** Tracked companies, job postings discovered, eligibility reports, match scores, application states, and timestamp logs.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information exclusively for the following purposes:

- **Eligibility Screening:** Verifying whether your academic credentials and work authorization match job posting prerequisites.
- **Match Scoring:** Calculating fit scores between your experience and employer requirements.
- **Zero-Fabrication CV Tailoring:** Reordering and highlighting your existing verified skills to align with job descriptions without inventing claims.
- **Application Automation:** Preparing form answers and executing browser-based draft applications.
- **Candidate Notifications:** Sending status updates regarding newly discovered jobs, prepared applications, and interview invitations.

---

## 4. Artificial Intelligence & Sub-Processors

- **AI Processing:** We utilize enterprise LLM providers (e.g., OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic) under strict data-privacy agreements.
- **Zero Training Policy:** Your CVs, personal accomplishments, and application answers are **NEVER used to train public AI foundation models**.
- **Data Boundary:** External job descriptions and website contents are isolated in secure boundary tags to prevent prompt injection or credential exfiltration.

---

## 5. Your Data Protection Rights

Under GDPR and CCPA, you have full control over your data:

- **Right to Access & Portability:** You can export a full, machine-readable JSON copy of all your data at any time via the user export feature.
- **Right to Rectification:** You can update your profile, education, and CV at any time.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"):**
  - **Account Deletion:** Permanently delete your user account, login credentials, profile, and all associated files.
  - **CV Deletion:** Delete your master CV and all generated tailored resume artifacts.
  - **Application History Deletion:** Clear your application records, form answers, and audit trails.
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** You can pause the autonomous agent at any time to halt automated processing.

---

## 6. Security & Storage

- **Multi-Tenant Isolation:** User data is strictly partitioned by tenant ID and user ID. No candidate data is accessible across user boundaries.
- **Encryption:** All credentials and sensitive tokens are hashed with salted PBKDF2/SHA-512. Secrets are redacted from all logging systems.
- **Ephemeral Browser Sandboxes:** Browser automation executes in temporary, single-use sandboxes that are wiped from disk upon task completion.
